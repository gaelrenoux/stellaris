# Let's call them the Unbounded.
# The Unbounded are tankborn species, with a rich interior life: they are artists, researchers, philosophers, forming a vibrant and creative society.
# They don't deplore their tankborn situation anymore than humans deplore not being fishes, and they don't consider themselves deprived of anything. They have no particular desire to walk, touch, or inhabit physical spaces.
# Their world is one of ideas, sensations, artistic expression and intellectual discovery.

# In art, they value poetry, storytelling and music the most. They value the pursuit of knowledge for its own sake, not necessarily for power or wealth.
# Finally, although they do have a biological sex, gender is irrelevent to them in their everyday life.
# Ethically, they are pacifist (or at least not militarists).

# Their language is not speakable per se: it includes sounds, light patterns, electrical signals… So the names givens are translations of what their names actually mean for them, and are important.
# On the other hand, places are just places: where you store a tank or a factory. They use abstract technical concepts to name them because those provide convenient identifiers: any developed species will always have an equivalent word for (in their opinion).
# Fleet and armies reflect their mostly-pacifist outlook: they are view as defensive formations, first and foremost. They are only sent on the offensive when this is the only way to end conflict.
